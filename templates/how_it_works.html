How BlindSpot works

Three simple steps between you and a clearer picture of your website's security.

  1. 1

    Enter a website URL

    Create a free account, then enter the address of a website you own — or have written permission to test. You'll confirm that permission before the scan runs.

  2. 2

    BlindSpot runs its checks

    BlindSpot performs a set of safe, read-only checks against the public parts of the website. It looks at headers, responses, and publicly reachable pages.

  3. 3

    Get a clear report

    You receive a simple security score and a list of findings. Each finding shows how serious it is, why it matters, and exactly what to do about it.

What BlindSpot does not do

  • No attacks. BlindSpot never tries to break into a website or exploit a vulnerability.
  • No password guessing. It doesn't attempt to log in or crack anything.
  • No downloads of your data. It only checks whether a resource is publicly reachable.
  • No scanning without permission. Authorized checks only — always.
